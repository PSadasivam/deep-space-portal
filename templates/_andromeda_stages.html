The research in five stages

Each stage answers one question, in an order chosen so that no claim gets ahead of its evidence: reproduce first, commit before the new data, then test and interpret, then update in public. Stages 1 and 2 had to finish before Gaia Data Release 4 on 2 December 2026. Andromeda’s motion from DR4 must be derived by astronomers after the release, which may take weeks to months, so Stage 5 waits for it.
